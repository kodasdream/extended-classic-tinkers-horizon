## Materials, traits and tool leveling

The materials and their stats come from the Tinkers setup in the Dungeons, Dragons and Space Shuttles 1.12.2 modpack, which draws on Tinkers' Construct, TAIGA (Tinkers Alloying Addon, by Zkaface and Chefe), PlusTiC (by Landmaster), Tinkers' Tool Leveling, Mystical Agriculture's Tinkers materials and more. Their trait names and descriptions are what those mods call them; their behaviour is rewritten here from those descriptions. No code or assets from TAIGA, PlusTiC or the other mods are used. If you made one of these and want a name or number changed, please open an issue.

No textures, code or data from Minecraft, Mojang or Microsoft are included.
